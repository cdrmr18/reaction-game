How it works

  1. Start a round with the button, a tap on the pad, or the spacebar.
  2. Wait while the pad says “Wait…”. The delay is random, between 2 and 5 seconds.
  3. When the pad says “GO!”, press as fast as you can.
  4. Pressing too early is a false start. No time is recorded.
